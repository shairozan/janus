# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Janus to be a project people can contribute to without having to brace
themselves first. Everyone participating — issues, pull requests, discussions — is
expected to treat others with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be civil and assume good faith. Most disagreements are a missing piece of
  context, not bad intent.
- Critique the code, the design, or the argument — not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and that they owe you a reason rather than
  a debate.
- Remember that contributors have varying experience with Go, with
  pharmacometrics, and with English. Someone unfamiliar with one is not therefore
  unfamiliar with the others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions — and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via a GitHub
[security advisory][advisories], which is the private channel this project has.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts for behaviour
they judge to breach this document.

[advisories]: https://github.com/shairozan/janus/security/advisories/new
